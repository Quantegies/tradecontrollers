Trading risk

Futures and forex trading contains substantial risk and is not suitable for every investor. An investor could potentially lose all or more than the initial investment. Risk capital is money that can be lost without jeopardizing one's financial security or lifestyle. Only risk capital should be used for trading and only those with sufficient risk capital should consider trading. Past performance is not necessarily indicative of future results.

Hypothetical performance

Hypothetical performance results have many inherent limitations. No representation is being made that any account will or is likely to achieve profits or losses similar to those shown. In fact, there are frequently sharp differences between hypothetical performance results and actual results achieved by any trading program.

Hypothetical trading does not involve financial risk and therefore cannot fully account for the impact of financial risk in actual trading. The ability to withstand losses or adhere to a trading plan despite losses are material factors that may affect real trading results.

Virtual currency

Trading digital assets or derivatives tied to digital assets may involve additional risk. Users should review official CFTC advisories and regulatory guidance regarding virtual currency trading before participating in such markets.
