Is TradeControllers an auto-trader?

No. It helps you place and manage orders deliberately. It does not provide signals or trade for you.

Which platforms does it support?

It is designed for Tradovate and NinjaTrader web workflows through a Chrome extension.

How do I get started?

After purchase, check your email for setup steps and an access key.

Will it make me profitable?

No tool can guarantee trading results. TradeControllers helps you follow your own process; trading still involves substantial risk.

Does it work with prop firms?

It is designed for trading environments that support Tradovate or NinjaTrader web. Check your firm's rules and platform setup before use.

Where can I get help?

Join the TradeControllers Discord for setup and support.
